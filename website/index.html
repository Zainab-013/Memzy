About Us

Simplifying How You Remember

Memzy was created out of a simple frustration: saving links to WhatsApp self-chats, writing ideas in scattered notes apps, and screenshotting things that get buried in our galleries.

Our mission is to build a unified space that fits your existing habits. We believe the easiest interface is a chat window. By combining a conversational interface with local natural language parsing, Memzy converts casual typing into structured, actionable memory notes.

"We designed Memzy to be beautiful, fast, and above all, completely offline. Your personal memories are yours alone."
